## Sources and citation

Use original papers as the authority for scientific claims; the summaries and categories below are editorial annotations, not paper abstracts. Dates describe the earliest dated public version verified during curation, usually the arXiv v1 submission date, not a verified announcement timestamp. An earlier conference presentation can have a different date from the linked arXiv version.
