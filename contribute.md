      <article>
        <h2>Which papers fit?</h2>
        <p>Methods for reasoning without writing every step, and studies that test or explain them. We also include related ways to generate reasoning, such as parallel or diffusion-based methods.</p>
        <p>For work on hidden states, memory, or diffusion, explain the connection to reasoning. Earlier architectural and theoretical background is listed separately.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h2>What to send</h2>
        <ul class="lit-check-list">
          <li>A link to the paper on arXiv, OpenReview, or the publisher's site</li>
          <li>A sentence explaining why it belongs</li>
          <li>A code link, if available</li>
        </ul>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h2>What happens next?</h2>
        <p>We check the paper's details and choose the relevant categories. A paper can appear in more than one category. You do not need to choose the labels yourself.</p>
        <p>The list is maintained by <a href="https://yuntiandeng.com/">Yuntian Deng</a>.</p>
      </article>
